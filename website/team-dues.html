Dues

Every due in one place.

A team admin adds a due in under a minute, and everyone on it sees what they owe.

  • What it's for

    A name, the amount, an optional due date, and a note everyone on it sees, like "Includes socks and a hat".

  • Who owes it

    On a team, a due is for the players, each checked to start. In a group, it's for the members.

  • Changes mid-season

    Add a player who joins late, waive someone, or change the amount; every payment already recorded stays.

Partial payments

Paid a part? Record it.

Record each payment as the money comes in, any amount up to what's left, with the day and a short note like "Venmo" or "Cash at practice". Each share reads Owes, Partly paid, Paid, or Waived, and a mistaken payment can be undone.

For admins

Who owes what, at a glance.

The Dues screen opens with what's owed in all, what's paid, and what's left. Who owes what lists each person, who owes the most first, with every due they're on and where each one stands.

How to pay

Person to person, no fees.

Add the team's Venmo, Cash App, or Apple Cash, and a note on how to pay. People pay the team directly, and an admin records the payment once it arrives. Battleplan never takes, holds, or moves money, and charges nothing for dues.

Private

Private by design.

  • Admins see everything

    Only a team's admins see what everyone owes and has paid.

  • Everyone sees their own

    Everyone else sees only What you owe: their own, or, for a guardian, what their players owe.

  • Groups too

    In a group, each member sees only their own share.

How to track team dues

Ditch the spreadsheet.

Most teams track dues with a spreadsheet, a stack of Venmo screenshots, and "did I pay?" in the group chat. With Battleplan, the record lives with the team: admins update it as money comes in, guardians check what they owe for themselves, and nobody has to ask.

Dues are in the iPhone and iPad app, and in any modern browser on a phone or a computer.

FAQ

Questions

Does Battleplan collect payments?

No. People pay the team directly with Venmo, Cash App, or Apple Cash, and an admin records each payment once it arrives. Battleplan never takes, holds, or moves money.

Are there fees?

No. Dues are free, like everything in Battleplan, and since no money goes through us, we take no cut.

Can people pay part now and the rest later?

Yes. Admins record each payment as it comes in, any amount up to what's left, and the share reads Partly paid until it's paid in full.

Who can see what I owe?

You and your team's admins, and, for a player's dues, the player and their guardians. Nobody else sees what anyone owes or has paid.

Can a group track dues?

Yes. In a group, each due is for the members you pick, and each member sees only their own.

Are dues in the iPhone app?

Yes. Dues are in the iPhone and iPad app, and in any modern browser on a phone or a computer.
