Announcements and DMs

Say it to everyone, or to one person.

The message everyone has to see stays where everyone sees it.

  • Pinned messages

    Team admins pin up to three messages to a bar at the top of the chat: the field change, the tournament hotel, the uniform rule.

  • Announcements

    An announcement stands out in the chat and notifies the whole team, even people set to Important only.

  • @everyone

    Admins can @mention everyone on the team for a quick heads-up that reaches them like a mention.

  • Direct messages

    Message anyone you share a team with, one to one. Only the two of you see the conversation.

Polls

Settle it without forty replies.

Ask the team right in the chat: which weekend works, what color the new jerseys should be, who's bringing the cooler. A poll takes one choice or several, named or anonymous, and can close on its own at a time you pick.

  • One choice or several
  • Anonymous voting
  • Closes on its own

One app, not three

Chat, schedule, and plays together.

No more jumping between a chat app, a calendar, and a spreadsheet.

  • Games in the chat

    Post a game or practice to the chat as a card when you add it, and people answer Going, Maybe, or Not going right on the card. See schedules and RSVPs.

  • Plays in the chat

    Anyone on the team can post a saved play to the chat, where a tap opens it step by step. See the baseball and softball whiteboard.

  • Photos in one place

    Every photo shared in the chat is also in Documents, newest first, so the team photo is easy to find.

  • Notifications your way

    Choose how much each team notifies you, or mute it. At Important only, replies to you, mentions, and announcements still come through.

Made for teams

Built for a team, not an office.

  • Invite by link or QR code

    Share the team's invite link or QR code, add people by email, or give out the team's invite code.

  • Report and block

    Report a message or a person to the team's admins and to Battleplan; the person is never told who reported them. Block someone, and their messages collapse and stop notifying you.

  • Accounts for adults

    Accounts are for adults 18 and older. Players who don't use the app are on the roster, and their guardians answer for them.

  • Groups too

    Not a team? A group gets the same chat, and everyone in it answers for themselves.

Bring your history

Bring your GroupMe chat.

A team admin can import a GroupMe group's messages, photos, files, and events, with everyone's GroupMe names and the original dates. Nothing imported sends notifications, so nobody's phone blows up.

On every phone and computer.

FAQ

Questions

Is Battleplan's team chat free?

Yes. Every team and group gets the chat and every other feature, with no ads and no paid tier. Each team has room for 200 MB of photos and files.

Can a group that isn't a team use it?

Yes. Create a group instead of a team, for a club, a league's volunteers, or friends who play on Sundays. Groups get the same chat, schedule, polls, documents, and dues, and everyone answers for themselves. Groups has more.

Can we bring our GroupMe chat?

Yes. A team admin exports the group from GroupMe, then imports it in Team settings → Import. Messages keep everyone's names and the original dates. Switch to Battleplan has the steps.

Does everyone need an account?

Everyone who chats needs one, and accounts are for adults 18 and older. Players who don't use the app are on the roster, and their guardians answer for them.

Can I message one person instead of the whole team?

Yes. Direct messages are one to one with anyone you share a team with, and only the two of you see them.
