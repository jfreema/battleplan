Questions

Is Battleplan really free?

Yes. Every team gets every feature, with no ads. Each team has room for 200 MB of photos and files.

Is Battleplan a team chat app?

Yes, and more. Every team and group gets a group chat with photos, replies, reactions, @mentions, pinned announcements, and direct messages, beside its schedule, polls, documents, and dues. Team chat has the details.

Does every player need an account?

No. Accounts are for adults 18 and older. Coaches add the players, and guardians answer for them. Adult players answer for themselves.

How do people join my team?

Share the team's invite link or QR code, or add people by email. People can also type in the team's invite code.

Does it sync with Google Calendar?

Yes. A private calendar link keeps Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook up to date with your team's games and practices, including new times and cancellations. Team schedule and RSVPs has more.

Can we draw plays?

Yes. Team admins draw plays on a baseball and softball diamond, a basketball court, a soccer pitch, a football field, or a blank board, add steps, and press play. Anyone on the team can post a saved play to the chat or save it as a GIF. See the baseball and softball whiteboard.

Does it work on iPhone, Android, and computers?

Yes. The iPhone and iPad app is on the App Store, and Battleplan works in any modern browser on a phone or a computer. You sign in with Google or Apple. The Android app is in testing: help test it.
