Your whole team, on the same page.

Battleplan is team chat and scheduling for sports teams. Talk as a team, plan games and practices, see who's coming, run quick polls, and keep the lineup and league rules where everyone can find them.

Everything a team needs. Keep everyone on the same page.

One place for the conversation, the calendar, and the paperwork, so nobody has to dig through a group text to find out when warmups start.

  1. Team chat

    One conversation for the whole team, with everyone's name on every message.

    • Share photos from practice and game day.
    • React with a quick thumbs-up, reply to a specific message, or @mention someone so they're sure to see it.
    • Coaches pin what matters to the top of the chat and send announcements that reach everyone.
    • New games, practices, and polls show up right in the chat.
    • Message a coach, a player, or a guardian one to one. Only the two of you see it.

  2. Schedule and RSVPs

    Games, practices, meetings, and team events in one schedule, with every detail people ask about.

    • Going, Maybe, or Not going in one tap, and a live count for the coach.
    • Opponent, home or away, directions to the field, and add to your calendar.
    • See every team you're on in one schedule.

  3. Polls

    Settle the jersey color, the tournament weekend, or where to eat after the game, without forty replies.

    • One choice or several, with results as people vote.
    • See who voted for what, or keep the poll anonymous.
    • Set a closing time, or close it whenever you're ready.

  4. Documents

    The lineup, the league rules, the waiver, the field directions: one tap away for everyone on the team.

    • Upload files or save links, like a sign-up form or the league website.
    • Pin the important ones to the top.
    • The team gets a heads-up when something new is added.

  5. Players and guardians

    Every team has a roster of players. It works for adult and youth teams alike.

    • Join and tap "I'm a player" to RSVP for yourself, or "I'm here for a player" to RSVP for them as their guardian.
    • Players who don't use the app don't need accounts. Their guardians answer for each one.
    • Coaches see which players are coming, with coaches and others counted apart.

  6. Plays

    Draw up plays on a real field: a baseball diamond, a basketball court, a soccer pitch, a football field, or a blank board. Made for an iPad at practice, and it works on a laptop too.

    • Drag players and the ball with a finger or a mouse to show how a play unfolds.
    • Add steps, then press play to watch everyone move into place.
    • Save the plays worth keeping, so the team can look at them anytime.

Questions

Is Battleplan really free?

Yes. Every team gets every feature at no cost, and there are no ads. Each team has room for 200 MB of photos and files, and admins can see what's using the space and free some up.

Does every player need an account?

No. Accounts are for adults 18 and older. The coach adds the players, and their guardians join and answer for them. Adult players can join and claim their own spot on the roster.

How do people join my team?

Create a team, then share its invite link or QR code, or add people by email. People can also type in the team's invite code.

How do I move my team over from another app?

A team admin does it, in Battleplan: open your team's menu, tap Import, and choose the app. You'll see a preview of everything before it's added. Nothing imported sends notifications, and importing again adds only what's new. RSVPs and attendance stay behind.

From GroupMe: messages, photos, files, and events
  1. On a computer, go to web.groupme.com and sign in.
  2. Click your profile picture, choose Export my data, then Create export. If GroupMe asks which chats to include, choose only your team's group.
  3. When it's ready, download it from Export my data: a .zip file. A large export comes in parts; import each one.
  4. In Battleplan, open Import, choose GroupMe, and choose the .zip file.
  5. Pick your team's group, choose what to bring (messages, with or without photos, documents, and events), check the preview, and tap Import.

If the export has all your GroupMe chats, that's fine: only the group you pick is imported, and your other chats never leave your device. Messages keep everyone's GroupMe names and the original dates. Likes, polls' votes, and videos aren't imported.

From TeamSnap: your schedule, players, and files
  1. Schedule: in TeamSnap on the web, open your team's Schedule, then Settings, then Sync Calendar / Export, and copy the calendar link. On an iPhone: Schedule, the share button, then Subscribe.
  2. Players: on the web, open your team's Roster, then Export, then Export CSV File.
  3. Files: open the Media tab, then Files, and download the ones you want.
  4. In Battleplan, open Import, choose TeamSnap, then paste the link, choose the roster file, and choose the files. Check the preview, and tap Import.

Players come with their names, numbers, and birthdates; coaches and contact details stay behind, and guardians link themselves to their players. TeamSnap doesn't export its chat.

From TeamReach: your schedule
  1. In TeamReach, open your team's schedule and choose the option to subscribe to it in a calendar app (iCal). Copy the calendar link.
  2. In Battleplan, open Import, choose TeamReach, paste the link, check the preview, and tap Import.
From GameChanger: your schedule
  1. In the GameChanger app, open your team and tap the gear, then Schedule Sync, Sync Schedule to Your Calendar, and Copy Calendar Link.
  2. In Battleplan, open Import, choose GameChanger, paste the link, check the preview, and tap Import.

Games come in with the opponent and home or away. Coaches, players, and guardians get every event in their link; followers get only games.

From Google Calendar: your events
  1. On a computer, open Google Calendar and go to Settings.
  2. Under Settings for my calendars, choose your team's calendar, then Integrate calendar, and copy the Secret address in iCal format.
  3. In Battleplan, open Import, choose Google Calendar, paste the address, check the preview, and tap Import.

Keep the secret address private: anyone who has it can see that calendar.

Can I use it on my computer?

Yes. Sign up or log in in your browser with your Google account. It's the same account you'll use in the phone apps.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account at the bottom. You can also ask us by email. See Delete your account for the details.
