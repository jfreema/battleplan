FAQ

Questions

Is Battleplan really free?

Yes. Every team gets every feature, with no ads. Each team has room for 200 MB of photos and files.

Does every player need an account?

No. Accounts are for adults 18 and older. Coaches add the players, and guardians answer for them. Adult players answer for themselves.

How do people join my team?

Share the team's invite link or QR code, or add people by email. People can also type in the team's invite code.

Does Battleplan handle payments for dues?

No. Admins track who has paid, and people pay the team directly with Venmo, Cash App, or Apple Cash. Only admins see what others owe.

How do I move my team from another app?

A team admin opens Team info (ⓘ at the top), taps Import, and picks GroupMe, TeamSnap, TeamReach, GameChanger, or Google Calendar. You see a preview first, and nothing imported sends notifications. Switch to Battleplan has the steps for each app.

Does it work on iPhone, Android, and computers?

Yes. The iPhone and iPad app is on the App Store, and Battleplan works in any modern browser on a phone or a computer. You sign in with Google or Apple. Dues and the new color schemes come to the iPhone app in its next update; they're in the web app now. An Android app is on the way.

Can I change how my team looks?

Yes, for you. Pick Army green, Light, Dark, Navy, or Light blue for each team in Team info, and set your default on the Teams screen. Everyone picks their own.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. You can also ask us by email. Delete your account has the details.
