Help test Battleplan on Android

Battleplan's Android app is in a closed test on Google Play before it opens to everyone, and Google asks for testers who keep it for 14 days.

Who can test

  • You're 18 or older.
  • You live in the United States or Canada.
  • You have an Android phone with the Google Play Store.

How to join

  1. Email support@getbattleplan.app with your name, the email address you use for Google Play, and your phone's model.
  2. We add you to the tester list and email you a link to opt in.
  3. Open the link on your phone, tap to become a tester, then install Battleplan from Google Play.
  4. Stay opted in for at least 14 days, and use it with a team or group as you normally would. If something breaks or looks wrong, tell us at support@getbattleplan.app.

Email us to join

We use your email address only to add you to the test, as the Privacy Policy says.

Not on Android?

The iPhone and iPad app is on the App Store, and Battleplan works in any modern browser on a phone or a computer.
