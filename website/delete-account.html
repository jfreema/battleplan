Delete your Battleplan account

You can delete your account yourself in the app at any time, or ask us to do it by email.

In the app

  1. Open Battleplan on your phone, or in your browser, and sign in.
  2. Tap your picture in the top corner, then Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Read what will happen, confirm, and sign in again to prove it's you.

If you own a team, it passes to someone else when your account is deleted (see below). To pick the new owner yourself, transfer ownership in the team's settings first.

By email

If you can't get into the app, email support@getbattleplan.app with the subject "Delete my account". Send it from the email address on your Battleplan account so we can tell it's you; if you can't, we'll ask you to confirm another way. We'll reply to confirm before we delete anything, and again when it's done.

What happens when you delete your account

  • Deleted: your profile (name, photo, contact email, and preferences), your sign-in methods, your team memberships, your RSVPs for upcoming events, your notifications, and your list of blocked people.
  • Teams you own pass to the team's longest-serving admin, otherwise its longest-serving member, unless you transfer ownership first. A team with nobody else on it is archived.
  • Youth teams: you stop being a parent or guardian of any players. A player left with no guardian stays on the roster for the coach to manage.
  • Your past messages and the documents you shared stay with your teams so the conversation still makes sense, but they're shown from "Deleted user" instead of your name. If you want any of them gone too, delete them in the app before you delete your account, or ask a team admin.
  • Timing: deleted data is removed from our database right away, and from our backups within 7 days.

Deleting your account can't be undone. If you only want to leave one team, open that team's menu (⋯) and choose Leave team instead.
