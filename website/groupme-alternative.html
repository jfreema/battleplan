Your history

Keep your chat history.

A team admin imports your GroupMe export: messages, photos, files, and events, with everyone's GroupMe names and the original dates. Only the group you pick comes over, nothing imported sends notifications, and importing again adds only what's new. Likes, polls' votes, and videos aren't imported.

Switching

Move in three steps.

It takes a few minutes, most of it waiting for GroupMe's export.

  1. On a computer, sign in at web.groupme.com, click your profile picture, choose Export my data, then Create export.
  2. When it's ready, download the .zip file from Export my data.
  3. In Battleplan, open Team settings (the gear at the top) → Import, choose GroupMe and the file, pick your group, check the preview, and tap Import.

FAQ

Questions

Does our chat history come with us?

Yes. Messages, photos, files, and events come over with everyone's GroupMe names and the original dates. Likes, polls' votes, and videos don't.

Is Battleplan free?

Yes. Every team and group gets every feature, with no ads and no paid tier.

Does everyone need the app?

Everyone who chats needs a free account, in the iPhone or iPad app or in any modern browser. Battleplan doesn't send the chat by text message. An Android app is on the way.

Will the import set off everyone's notifications?

No. Nothing imported sends notifications.

Can a group that isn't a team switch too?

Yes. Create a group instead of a team, then import the same way, in Group settings → Import.
