Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Battleplan is team chat and scheduling for sports teams. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • No ads. We don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • What you share in a team (messages, photos, schedule, documents) is visible to that team's members. A direct message is visible only to you and the person you send it to.
  • The people who run Battleplan can access team content for moderation and support, and every time they do, it's recorded. They see a direct message only if it's reported.
  • Accounts are for adults. The whole team sees only a player's name, jersey number, and walk-up song. Optional details such as a date of birth, emergency contacts, and notes are seen only by the team's admins and the people linked to that player.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the app or by email, and ask us for a copy of your data.

Who we are

Battleplan is run by Jared Freeman, of Louisiana ("Battleplan", "we", "us"). This policy covers the Battleplan apps for iPhone and Android, the web app at getbattleplan.app/app, this website, and the emails we send.

What we collect

From you

  • Account and profile: your name, email address, and profile photo, plus your settings (theme and notification choices). When you first sign in with Google or Apple, we copy your name and email from that account (and your photo, from Google) to start your profile. Apple shares your name only the first time you use it with Battleplan. After that, only you change your profile. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we get Apple's relay address, not your real one.
  • Sign-in: which sign-in methods you use (Google, Apple, or email and password). Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider (Firebase Authentication); we never see or store them.
  • Confirmations: when you confirmed you're 18 or older and agreed to the Terms of Use.
  • What you share with your teams, and in direct messages: messages, replies, photos, reactions, polls and votes, events, RSVPs and their notes, and documents and links. The app removes location data from photos before uploading them.
  • Contact info (optional): a phone number and email address you choose to share with a team. You can change or remove them at any time.
  • Reports and blocks: the reports you file (what you reported, the reason, and any details you add) and the people you block.
  • Messages to us: what you send when you email support, and our replies.

From other people

  • Added by a team admin: a team admin can add you to their team by entering your name and email address. That creates an account for you and sends you a setup email. Until you set it up, you appear on the roster as "Not joined yet", and admins can answer RSVPs for you.
  • Invites: when someone invites you by email, we store your email address with the invite.
  • Players: any team member can add a player to the team's roster with a name, and optionally a jersey number. A player added by a member who isn't a team admin waits for an admin to approve it. A player can be linked to the player's own account, when the player is an adult who uses Battleplan, and to the accounts of the player's parents or guardians. A team admin can add a player with the player's email address, or their parents' or guardians' email addresses, which adds those people as described above.
  • Player details (optional): a team admin, or someone linked to the player (the player themselves or a parent or guardian), can add the player's date of birth, up to two emergency contacts (each a name, phone number, and relationship), and notes for coaches. We don't collect health information.
  • Imported from other apps: a team admin can bring their team's history over from another app. From GroupMe's data export, that's the group's messages with each writer's GroupMe name and when they posted, and the group's photos, shared files, and events. From TeamSnap, it's the team's events, the players' names, jersey numbers, and birthdates from its roster file (not their contact details), and files the admin downloaded from TeamSnap. From TeamReach's, GameChanger's, or Google Calendar's calendar link, it's the team's events. Imported messages show who wrote them in the other app and aren't linked to anyone's Battleplan account. We keep imported content like anything else shared with the team.

Automatically

  • Devices: a notification token for each phone or browser you're signed in on, with its platform (iPhone, Android, or web) and app version, so we can send you notifications.
  • Usage analytics: on iPhone and Android, Google Analytics for Firebase records how the app is used, such as app opens and screens, with device and app details (model, operating system, app version, and approximate country from your IP address). It never includes what you write, and it doesn't use advertising IDs.
  • Crash and error reports: technical details when something goes wrong (what failed, the device and app version) so we can fix it. On iPhone and Android these go to Firebase Crashlytics. In the web app, a sample of errors is sent to our own servers with your account ID.
  • Security and abuse prevention: checks that requests come from the real Battleplan apps (Firebase App Check), counters that enforce limits like how many invites you can send a day, and your account's last-active time.
  • Admin records: a log of actions by team admins and by the people who run Battleplan (who did what, to what, and when), including each time we open a team's content.

How we use it

We use your information only to run Battleplan:

  • to provide the service: show your teams their chat, schedule, polls, and documents, deliver messages, send reminders and notifications, and send the emails you need (setup links, invites, RSVP emails a team admin sends to people who haven't answered for their players, and security notices such as "a sign-in method was added to your account");
  • to keep accounts and teams safe: verify sign-ins, prevent spam and abuse, enforce limits, and review reports, including emailing the people who run Battleplan about each new report, new account, and new or deleted team (with the name and email address of the person or team owner, never what was reported or who reported it);
  • to answer support requests;
  • to find and fix bugs and understand which features are used, so we can improve the app;
  • to comply with the law.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similar effects.

Who can see it

  • Your teams: what you share in a team is visible to that team's members, along with your name, photo, and title, and any phone number and email you chose to share with that team. Teams are walled off from each other: members of one team can't see your other teams or what you share there. Your profile shows people only the teams you share with them.
  • Direct messages are visible only to you and the person you're talking with, someone you share a team with.
  • RSVP emails: the answer links in an RSVP email work only for that event and the players it names, until the event starts, without signing in, so anyone you forward the email to could answer with them.
  • Calendar links: if you make a calendar link for a team's schedule, the calendar service you add it to (such as Google, Apple, or Microsoft) reads the schedule through it, and so can anyone you give the link to: the events' names, times, places, and notes, never anyone's name or RSVP. You can reset or turn off a link in the app at any time.
  • Team admins manage their team. They can see the roster, including who hasn't joined yet, answer RSVPs for people who haven't joined, see the team's reports, and remove messages, photos, documents, and members.
  • Players and guardians: a member linked to a player, as the player or as a parent or guardian, answers RSVPs for that player and can correct the player's name, number, and walk-up song. A player waiting for approval is visible only to the team's admins and the member who added it.
  • Player details (date of birth, emergency contacts, and notes) are visible only to the team's admins and the members linked to that player. Other team members can't see them, or whether a player has any. Super admins can see them for support but can't change them. They never appear in chat, notifications, or search.
  • The people who run Battleplan ("super admins") can access any team's content, including messages, photos, and documents, and account details such as your email address. They use this only for moderation and support: for example, to act on a report of abuse or to help with a request you sent. They don't read direct messages: when someone reports one, they see what was reported, to act on it. Every time a super admin opens a team or takes an action, it's recorded in an audit log.
  • Companies that help us run Battleplan, listed below, process data for us under contract and only to provide their service.
  • The law: we may disclose information if the law requires it (for example, a valid subpoena or court order), or if we believe in good faith that it's needed to protect someone's safety or to protect Battleplan and its users from fraud or abuse.
  • A new owner: if Battleplan is ever transferred to another person or company (including a company formed by its current owner), your information would move with it and stay covered by this policy's commitments. We'd tell you first.

We don't share your information with anyone else.

Companies that help us run Battleplan

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): sign-in, database, file storage, server functions, push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), app settings (Remote Config), app verification (App Check), crash reports (Crashlytics), and usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase). Read Firebase's privacy information.
  • Apple: push notifications to iPhones (Apple Push Notification service), and Sign in with Apple if you use it.
  • Google sign-in, if you use it.
  • Resend: sends Battleplan's emails, such as setup links, invites, security notices, and the notices to the people who run Battleplan described above.
  • Google reCAPTCHA in the web app, which checks that requests come from a real browser and not a bot (see Cookies and browser storage).

Apple's App Store and Google Play handle app downloads under their own privacy policies; we don't get personal information about you from them.

No ads, no selling, no tracking

  • There are no ads in Battleplan.
  • We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for targeted advertising.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, so the app never asks for permission to track you.
  • Our analytics run without advertising IDs, and we don't link analytics to your name or email.

If we ever decide to show ads, we'll update this policy and tell you before anything changes, and any ads would follow the rules that protect children's privacy.

Players and children

  • Battleplan accounts are for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly let anyone younger create an account, and if we learn that one has, we'll delete it.
  • Players don't get accounts of their own. An adult player can use their own account, linked to their player as that player. Players whose records are managed by others, such as parents, guardians, or coaches, may be children: they never sign in, and we never collect information directly from children.
  • Any team member can add a player, and a team admin approves it before the rest of the team sees it. What the whole team sees of a player is only a name, jersey number, and walk-up song: no photos, birthdates, or contact details. Optional details for coaches (a date of birth, emergency contacts, and notes) are visible only to the team's admins and the people linked to the player.
  • A player's record is visible only to that team's members. The team's admins and the members linked to the player (the player themselves, or a parent or guardian) can change it and its details, and a team admin can remove a player, which deletes its details too. A parent or guardian can also email privacy@getbattleplan.app to have their child's record removed.
  • Photos shared in a team's chat may show children. They're visible only to that team's members, admins can delete them, and anyone on the team can report one. Please share photos of other people's children only with their parents' permission.

Where your data is stored

Battleplan's database, files, and server functions run on Google Cloud in the United States (the us-central1, Iowa, region). Some of our providers, such as Google for analytics and crash reports and Resend for email, may process data in other places. If you use Battleplan from outside the United States, your information is transferred to and stored in the United States.

How long we keep it

  • Your profile and sign-in details: until you delete your account.
  • What you share in a team: until its author, a team admin, or the team's deletion removes it. Deleting your account doesn't remove your past messages and documents; they stay with the team, shown from "Deleted user" (see Delete your account).
  • Direct messages: until their sender deletes them. If you delete your account, the other person keeps the conversation, with your messages shown from "Deleted user"; a conversation neither person is left in is deleted.
  • Deleted items are erased from our database and file storage right away, and from our backups within 7 days. A deleted message leaves only a "This message was deleted" placeholder, with no text or photos. The one exception is a report: see Reports below.
  • Player details: until someone who can edit them removes them, or the player or the team is deleted. Removing a guardian or deleting a linked account doesn't delete them, because they belong to the player.
  • Archived teams are kept, read-only, until the owner restores or deletes them.
  • Notification-center items: 90 days.
  • Notification tokens: until you sign out on that device or delete your account, or after 60 days without use.
  • Limit counters: 2 days.
  • Admin and audit records: 1 year.
  • Reports: as long as the team exists. While a report is open, it keeps a copy of what was reported (up to 500 characters of a message's text, or a document's name) for the team's admins and us to review, even if the item itself is deleted. That copy is erased when the report is resolved.
  • Analytics: 2 months, Google Analytics' retention setting for user-level data.
  • Crash and error reports: about 90 days in Crashlytics, and 30 days in our server logs.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to help you, and no longer than 2 years.

Keeping it safe

  • All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and Google encrypts it where it's stored.
  • Database and storage rules let people see only the teams they belong to, and our servers check that requests come from the apps we publish (Firebase App Check).
  • Nobody at Battleplan, and no team admin, can see or set your password. Admins can only send setup and password-reset emails.
  • Changes to your sign-in methods, email, or password send a notice to your email address.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account) or by emailing us. Delete your account explains the steps and what happens to your messages, teams, and players. You can also delete your own messages and documents at any time, and a team's owner can delete the whole team, which erases everything in it.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: you can see and edit your profile in Settings. Team admins, and the members linked to a player, can correct the player's name, number, and walk-up song, and see, correct, or delete the player's details.
  • Get a copy: email privacy@getbattleplan.app and we'll send you a copy of your profile and the content you've shared, in a common file format.
  • Delete: delete your account in the app, or ask us by email.
  • Notifications: choose which notifications you get, per team and overall, in Settings, or turn them off in your phone's or browser's settings. We send only the emails the service needs; there are no marketing emails.
  • Blocking: block someone to hide their messages from you and stop notifications from them, in your teams and in direct messages. You can't send a direct message to someone you've blocked.

Depending on where you live (for example, California, other US states, the European Union, or the United Kingdom), the law may give you more rights, such as to object to or restrict how we use your information, or to complain to a data protection authority. We honor the requests above for everyone, wherever they live. We answer requests within 30 days, and we may ask you to confirm the request from your account's email address so we know it's you. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Because we don't sell or share personal information for advertising, there's nothing to opt out of. We treat browser "Global Privacy Control" and "Do Not Track" signals the same way: we don't track you either way.

Cookies and browser storage

  • This website: the pages on getbattleplan.app outside the web app use no cookies, analytics, or third-party scripts.
  • The web app at /app keeps you signed in and saves a copy of recent team data and your settings in your browser's own storage, so it opens quickly and works on a spotty connection. You can clear it at any time in your browser's settings. It uses no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Google reCAPTCHA runs in the web app to check that requests come from a real browser. It may set its own cookies and collect information about your browser and device, under Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change matters, for example if we start collecting something new or using it in a new way, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data: privacy@getbattleplan.app. Anything else: support@getbattleplan.app.
