The short version

  • No ads. We don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps and websites.
  • Your team's messages, photos, schedule, and documents are visible to your team's members.
  • Battleplan's super admins can access team content for moderation and support.
  • Accounts are for adults. On youth teams, adults enter only a player's name, jersey number, and position.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the app or by email.

What we collect

  • Account and profile: your name, email address, and profile photo.
  • What you share with your teams: messages, photos, files and links, reactions, poll votes, events, and RSVPs.
  • Youth teams: the player details adults enter: a player's name, jersey number, and position.
  • Devices: device tokens, so we can send notifications to the phones and browsers you're signed in on.
  • Usage analytics: how the app is used, collected without advertising IDs.
  • Crash reports: technical details when the app crashes, so we can fix it.

Who can see it

  • Your teams: what you share in a team is visible to that team's members. Your name and photo appear next to what you post.
  • Team admins manage their team: for example, they can see the roster and remove messages and documents.
  • Battleplan's super admins can access team content, including messages, photos, and documents, for moderation and support, for example to act on a report of abuse or to help with a support request.

[Final wording: service providers, legal requests, and any other cases.]

No ads, no selling, no tracking

  • There are no ads in Battleplan.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, so the app never asks for permission to track you.
  • Our analytics run without advertising IDs.

Youth teams and children

  • Battleplan accounts are for adults 18 and older.
  • Players on youth teams never sign in or get accounts. Coaches and guardians enter only a player's name, jersey number, and position: no photos, birthdates, or contact details.
  • Photos shared in a team's chat may show children. They're visible only to that team's members, and anyone on the team can report one.

How long we keep it

  • What you share stays until its author, a team admin, or the team's deletion removes it.
  • Deleted content is erased from our database right away and from our backups within 7 days.
