Bring your team

Bring your TeamSnap team.

A team admin imports from TeamSnap in a few steps, and sees a preview of everything before it's added.

  1. Schedule: copy your team's calendar link from TeamSnap's Sync Calendar / Export.
  2. Players: export your roster from TeamSnap as a CSV file.
  3. Files: download the ones you want from TeamSnap's Media tab.
  4. In Battleplan, open Team info → Import, choose TeamSnap, add the link and the files, check the preview, and tap Import.

Players come with their names, numbers, and birthdates; coaches and contact details stay behind, and guardians link themselves to their players. TeamSnap doesn't export its chat. See every step.

Straight answers

What Battleplan doesn't do.

Battleplan is for running a team, not a league. It doesn't take payments or registrations, keep live scores or stats, or stream games. Dues are tracked, not collected: people pay the team directly with Venmo, Cash App, or Apple Cash. If your team needs those, keep the tool that does them, and use Battleplan for the chat, the schedule, and the plays.

FAQ

Questions

Is Battleplan really free?

Yes. Every team gets every feature, with no ads and no paid tier. Each team has room for 200 MB of photos and files.

What comes over from TeamSnap?

Your schedule, from TeamSnap's calendar link; your players, with their names, numbers, and birthdates, from a roster export; and the files you download. Coaches, contact details, and the chat stay behind.

Does Battleplan handle payments or registration?

No. Battleplan tracks dues, but people pay the team directly with Venmo, Cash App, or Apple Cash. It has no registration forms.

Does it work on iPhone, Android, and computers?

The iPhone and iPad app is on the App Store, and Battleplan works in any modern browser on a phone or a computer. You sign in with Google or Apple. An Android app is on the way.

Can we use it with GameChanger?

Yes. Battleplan doesn't keep scores, but it imports a GameChanger schedule from its calendar link, with each game's opponent and home or away. Switch to Battleplan has the steps.
