# Battleplan is free

**Every team gets every feature, free, with no ads.** There is no paid plan. A paid "Plus" plan was planned on 2026-10-11 and dropped on 2026-10-12: store fees and Apple's rule that anything unlocked in the iPhone app must also be sold through in-app purchase (App Store Review Guideline 3.1.3(b)) made it costly, and locked features would slow down teams adopting the app.

## What every team gets

- Chat with photos, replies, links, and 16 reactions (👍 ❤️ 👎 😂 🔥 👏 🎉 💯 🙏 😮 😢 😡 ⚾ 🏀 ⚽ 🏆).
- Editing your own messages.
- A custom chat look: message color and background, set by the team's admins.
- The schedule and RSVPs, polls, documents, and notifications.
- Youth teams with guardians and per-player RSVPs.
- Every safety feature: report, block, moderation, and account deletion.

Later, also free: pinned messages, announcements, attendance history and exports, and a branded invite page (SPEC §9, v1.1 backlog).

## Storage

Storage is the cost that grows with every photo and file, so each team has a limit (SPEC §6.14, STORE-1 to STORE-6):

| | |
|---|---|
| Team storage | **200 MB** (photos, documents, the team logo, and the chat background; 500 MB until 2026-10-15) |
| Largest file | **10 MB** |
| Warning | At **90%**, admins see a warning and get a notification |
| Full | New uploads, attachments, and scheduling new events stop until space is freed. Nothing else turns off, and nothing is deleted automatically. |
| Freeing space | Team menu → **Media** lists every photo and file, largest first, for admins to delete |
| More space | A super admin can raise one team's limit. This isn't announced or advertised. |

For scale: a chat photo is resized to about 0.3 to 1 MB, so 200 MB holds roughly 200 to 600 photos.

## Ads, later

Ads may come once enough teams use Battleplan to justify the work (ROADMAP "Ads, later"). Youth teams mean parents and guardians use the app on behalf of children, so any ads plan has to account for children's privacy rules first: COPPA in the US, and Google Play's Families policy if the app is in the Families program or targets children. Until then, the Privacy Policy and store disclosures say there are no ads and no tracking (SPEC LEGAL-2).
